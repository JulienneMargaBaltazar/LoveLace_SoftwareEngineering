## Execution Flow by Phase

1. **Sprint Kickoff & Carryover Alignment (`SM`)** — `S3-SM-01` (#138), `S3-SM-02` (#139)  
   Establish the Sprint 3 board, lock owners/due dates/acceptance criteria, and triage any Sprint 2 carryover before core execution starts.
2. **Database & UI/UX Foundations (`DBA` & `UIX` — Parallel Tracks)** — `S3-DBA-01` (#116) to `S3-DBA-05` (#120) and `S3-UIX-01` (#131) to `S3-UIX-04` (#134), `S3-UIX-06` (#136), `S3-UIX-07` (#137)  
   Build HR views, duplicate-date validation, `app_user.empno` linkage, delete checks, and demo data alongside wireframes, job timeline design, hi-fi mockups, restricted-visibility states, and modals/dialogs.
3. **HR Feature Implementation & Access/Audit Wiring (`DEV`)** — `S3-DEV-01` (#106) to `S3-DEV-09` (#114 and #115)  
   Implement Department and Job CRUD, Employee search/filter/forms/soft-delete/separation, Job History management, Employee profile timeline, `View Job History` visibility gating, and `audit_user()` + per-action HR permission checks.
4. **Database Hardening, Audit Verification & Mid-Sprint Governance (`DBA` & `SM`)** — `S3-DBA-06` (#121), `S3-DBA-07` (#122), `S3-SM-03` (#140), `S3-SM-04` (#141)  
   Add indexes and backup/restore scripts, verify `audit_log` triggers across all HR mutations, and conduct mid-sprint and PR/handoff audits.
5. **QA Testing, Usability Review & Bug Closure (`TST` & `UIX`)** — `S3-TST-01` (#123) to `S3-TST-07` (#130) and `S3-UIX-05` (#135)  
   Execute functional, job-history, permission, separation/backup smoke, and Sprint 1–2 regression tests, complete the usability review of built screens, and verify all bug fixes.
6. **Demo Prep, Metrics & Sprint Closeout (`SM`)** — `S3-SM-06` (#143), `S3-SM-05` (#142), `S3-SM-07` (#144)  
   Prepare the demo script and dry-run checklist, update velocity/burndown and Sprint 4 readiness, and publish the Sprint 3 review and retrospective report.
